# Whistle Desktop v0.1.2

A refinement of the native offline transcription workspace, with the existing dark palette and mint accents.

## Changes

- More room for the transcript, a quieter sidebar and language selector, and clearer input, focus, and button states.
- Distinct model and transcription status, visible audio shortcuts, a labeled shortcut menu, and clearer privacy information.
- Recording and processing time, cancellation that preserves confirmed text, and model/error recovery feedback.
- Incoming text follows the bottom until you scroll back or edit. The final phrase remains visible when transcription completes.
- F6 focuses the transcript; Tab and Shift+Tab move between controls.
- Locally embedded Phosphor icons, including the license in portable packages.

## Validation and limits

Formatting, strict Clippy, 15 model-independent tests, the native engine integration test, and the Windows x64 release build passed. UI checks cover a 1,000-paragraph transcript, editing/undo/copy, scrolling, keyboard navigation, processing/cancellation/error recovery, and the minimum window size. A real MP3 import was also rendered and inspected.

Physical microphone capture and OS permission behavior remain unverified on the development host. The synthetic long transcript tests editing behavior rather than speech-recognition accuracy. Other platforms still require their own builds and validation. Windows binaries remain unsigned.

The model, audio pipeline, supported languages, and fully offline operation are unchanged. See `VALIDATION.md` for test conditions and limitations, and `RELEASING.md` for packaging and publishing instructions.
